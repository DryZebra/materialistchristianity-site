# The 4D Time Snake

> **Axiom 1.4**: *A human being is not an isolated 3D slice of matter existing only in the present second. Geometrically, a human being is a 4D spacetime structure—a continuous "time snake" extending from birth to death. Morality is the structural audit of that 4D line to ensure it does not fracture or deform under load.*

---

## 1. The Fallacy of the 3D Snapshot

Modern consumer culture encourages people to view themselves as static **3D snapshots**:

$$ \text{Snapshot at } t_0: \quad \text{"I feel good right now, so my choices don't matter."} $$

This snapshot illusion allows people to pretend that past betrayals don't exist and that future consequences won't arrive. It is the logic of borrowing money you can't pay back, lying to get through today, or destroying relationships because "it felt right in the moment."

**Materialist Christianity replaces the 3D snapshot with 4D Spacetime Reality.**

```
 (Birth) t = 0 ════════════════════════════════════════════════════ (Death) t = N
                         THE 4D CONTINUOUS TIME SNAKE
```

In 4D physics (Minkowski spacetime), every object traces a **Worldline**:
* If you tell a lie at $t = 10$, that lie remains embedded in your 4D worldline forever.
* When you reach $t = 20$, the structural strain of that lie at $t = 10$ is still pulling on your frame.
* You cannot "delete" past physical motion; you can only compensate for it through course correction and grace.

---

## 2. Truth as 4D Geometric Continuity

When Axiom 1.2 defines **Truth as Continuity**, it is referring directly to this 4D geometric reality:

$$ \text{Continuity} = \int_{0}^{T} \left\| \frac{d\mathbf{x}}{dt} - \mathbf{v}_{\text{plumb}} \right\|^2 dt \longrightarrow 0 $$

A "True Man" is someone whose 4D worldline is straight, solid, and Plumb. 
* His actions at age 20 match his actions at age 40.
* His work in public matches his work in secret.
* His promises made yesterday carry weight tomorrow.

When a life sways violently off plumb—telling different stories, abandoning commitments, switching loyalties under pressure—the 4D time snake becomes jagged, kinked, and structurally weak. It snaps under the first major crisis.

## 3. Scriptural and Physical Parallels

* **The Book of Life (Revelation 20:12)**: In scriptural metaphor, books are opened and men are judged "according to their deeds." In materialist terms, the "Book" is the unalterable 4D physical record of your worldline written into the fabric of history.
* **"Before Abraham Was, I Am" (John 8:58)**: Jesus references the eternal, continuous reality of the Logos—the pattern of truth that exists across the entire temporal timeline, outlasting individual generations.
* **Einstein's Block Universe**: In modern general relativity, past, present, and future form a unified 4D spacetime block. Your choices leave a permanent geometric footprint in the universe.

## 4. Key Takeaways

1. **You Are Your Worldline**: Stop identifying with your fleeting 5-minute feelings. You are the total continuous shape of your labor, choices, and relationships across time.
2. **Every Choice Leaves Sediment**: You cannot "undo" a physical action, but you can weld a structural brace onto a bad joint through repentance and repair.
3. **Build a 4D Structure That Holds**: Live today so that the man you will be 10 years from now can look back at your worldline and find a solid foundation.
