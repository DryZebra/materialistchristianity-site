# Forensic Exegesis: Hamartia (ἁμαρτία)

> **Forensic Audit**: *In classical and Koine Greek, ἁμαρτία (hamartia) is an engineering and ballistic term. It is composed of the privative prefix α- (not) and μάρπτω (marpto - to grasp, hit, or reach). It literally means "missing the mark" or failing to make clean contact with the intended target.*

---

## 1. Etymological and Historical Baseline

Before medieval Latin theology translated *hamartia* into *peccatum* (which carried connotations of spiritual guilt, moral stain, and offense against divine majesty), the word had a precise material usage:

* **Homer (The Iliad)**: An archer shoots an arrow, and the wind carries it wide of the target. The bowman *hamartanein* (missed).
* **Aristotle (Poetics)**: A tragic hero makes an error in judgment or calculation (*hamartia*) that leads to systemic downfall.
* **Koine Greek Trade Specs**: A carpenter cuts a timber 2 inches short, causing the joint to fail. The cut is a *hamartia*.
