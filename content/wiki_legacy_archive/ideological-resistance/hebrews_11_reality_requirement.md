# Hebrews 11: The Reality Requirement

**Hebrews 11:6** is the basic rule for anyone who wants to actually get something done. It’s the "Technical Floor" for building a community. If you don't start with the **Physical Real**, you're just wasting your time on "Ghost-Stories."

## 1. Believing "That He Is"

The verse says: *"But without faith it is impossible to please him: for he that cometh to God must believe that he is..."*

*   **God is Real**: To believe "that He Is" isn't a religious feeling. It’s the technical recognition that **Objective Reality** exists. It means that the laws of the world—like gravity, the strength of a weld, or the consequences of a lie—are real things that you have to deal with.
*   **The Logic of the Flesh**: You can't pray away a bad foundation or a broken pipe. You have to believe that the world has rules, and you have to work within those rules. If you're stuck in a "theory" while your neighborhood is starving, you've failed the reality requirement.

## 2. The Rewarder: Real Results

The verse continues: *"...and that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him."*

*   **Survival as the Prize**: In the real world, the "Reward" for working with the truth is **Survival**. If you build a high-trust neighborhood where people look out for each other, you get the real-world result of a community that stays alive when the big systems fail.
*   **Seeking the Real**: "Diligently seeking him" means looking for the **Truth** in every situation. It’s about doing the audit, checking the receipts, and making sure the work is actually getting done.

## 3. Summary

To be a Materialist Christian is to have a "Yes that means Yes." We don't hide in abstract ideas. We work with the **Real World** that God built, and we take responsibility for the results. That's the only way to please the "God who Is."
